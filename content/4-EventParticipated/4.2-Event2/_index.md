My aim was to connect the AWS concepts I am learning with practical architectures and understand the requirements involved in bringing AI applications into enterprise use.

I attended the Watch Party at Bitexco in Ho Chi Minh City and watched the Hanoi program online. The following sections summarize notable topics from the event for my report.

### Key Highlights

#### Platform Engineering and Application Modernization on Amazon EKS

- **VPBank — Platform Engineering at Scale:** explored building Internal Developer Platforms on Amazon EKS to support engineering workflows. This helped me understand how a shared platform can standardize deployment practices and reduce repetitive work.
- **NTQ Solution — Legacy Migration to EKS:** discussed migrating NestJS applications from EC2/PM2 to EKS with GitOps. My takeaway was that modernization involves deployment processes, configuration management, and subsequent operations as well as infrastructure changes.

#### Choosing Appropriate Architectures and Managing Storage Costs

- **Cloud Kinetics — Don't use a Ferrari to Get Phở:** emphasized choosing architectures suited to the problem and sizing resources according to actual needs. It encouraged me to consider suitability, cost, and operational effort when comparing solutions.
- **GEM & SoftwareOne — Stop Overpaying for Storage:** addressed Amazon S3 Storage Classes and cost-effective data lifecycle management. I learned to consider access frequency and retention requirements before choosing a storage approach.

#### Workload Identity and Security

The **Beyond IAM Roles** topic introduced a broader perspective on workload identity and zero-trust security design. It highlighted the need to consider each component's identity, the resources it can access, and how interactions between components are controlled.

I want to explore this further while practicing IAM and deploying AWS applications, particularly systems with multiple services or agents that use tools.

#### AI in Software Development and Infrastructure Automation

- **G-AsiaPacific Vietnam — AI-DLC:** explored integrating AI into the software development lifecycle as a teammate. I noted the importance of using AI within a defined process, checking its output, and keeping responsibility for results clear.
- **Samsung R&D Center — Multi-Agents for IaC:** discussed using multiple AI agents for infrastructure deployment and automated IaC drift remediation. This introduced me to the problem of deployed configurations diverging from their declared state and the potential role of AI in detecting and addressing those differences.

#### Agent Orchestration and AI Cost Governance

- **VinUni — Agent Harness & Frameworks:** covered multi-agent orchestration with Strands, LangGraph, and Amazon Bedrock AgentCore. It gave me direction for exploring how processing flows, tools, and agent collaboration can be organized around complex requests.
- **VIB — Bedrock Cost Control:** addressed near real-time AI spending controls and financial governance at enterprise scale. It showed why budget monitoring and usage limits need consideration during application design.

### What I Learned

My main takeaway was the shift toward platforms that can operate over the long term. Cloud and AI applications need to be considered alongside observability, security, cost controls, and incident handling.

The EKS and GitOps examples helped me understand why enterprises standardize deployment workflows. Architecture choices still need to match the problem's scale and the team's capabilities. I want to practice explaining why a service is appropriate for a requirement.

For AI systems, quality depends on data, tool orchestration, review processes, and operational limits. AI innovation needs engineering discipline to produce reliable products.

### How I Plan to Apply These Lessons

- **Start architecture decisions with requirements:** establish the problem and expected scale before selecting AWS services.
- **Explore EKS and GitOps:** build on container fundamentals, deployment workflows, and configuration management.
- **Practice S3 data management:** classify data by access frequency, retention needs, and cost.
- **Strengthen IAM knowledge:** pay attention to least privilege and workload identities.
- **Review AI-assisted work:** check generated code, configurations, and explanations before using them.
- **Include cost in design criteria:** track resource usage and estimate budgets from the experimental stage.

### My Experience

Although the main event took place in Hanoi, watching online with other members at the Bitexco Watch Party in Ho Chi Minh City gave me an opportunity to learn in a community setting. The event's topics helped me connect my current cloud studies with concrete enterprise requirements.

Breakfast and conversations with other members were also memorable. The experience gave me more motivation to continue studying AWS and AI and gradually develop the thinking needed to build practical systems.

I appreciate AWS Vietnam and the supporting organizations for making enterprise Cloud and AI engineering experiences accessible to the community.
